# Homepage delivery variants

`nancy-couwen.png` remains unchanged. `nancy-couwen-480.webp` (57,314 bytes) and `nancy-couwen-960.webp` (205,136 bytes) are proportional browser-Canvas delivery encodes at WebP quality 0.92, without generative editing, retouching or changes to identity. The existing PNG is the picture fallback.

Only the homepage switches to these responsive variants. Its original width/height aspect ratio, alt translations, portrait composition and animations are preserved. The online school source was not changed in this audit.
